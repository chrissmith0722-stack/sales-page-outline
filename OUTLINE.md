# Digital product sales page outline

Fill each section; delete what you don't need. Keep above-the-fold ruthless.

## 1. Hero

- **Headline:** outcome in plain language (not feature soup)
- **Subhead:** who it's for + time-to-value ("set up in 10 minutes")
- **Primary CTA:** Buy / Get instant access — $[PRICE]
- **Trust crumb:** "Used by N buyers" / "Updated [MONTH YEAR]" / rating

## 2. Problem

- 3 bullets of current pain
- Cost of inaction (time, money, embarrassment)

## 3. Promise / transformation

- After using this, you will…
- Non-promises (what you won't claim)

## 4. What's inside

| Item | Format | Benefit |
|------|--------|---------|
| Core template | PDF/Notion | … |
| Bonus checklist | MD | … |
| Quickstart | 2-min video/PDF | … |

## 5. Who it's for / not for

**For:** …  
**Not for:** … (builds trust)

## 6. Proof

- Screenshots / mockups
- Testimonials (name + context)
- Mini case: "Went from A → B"

## 7. Objection crushers

- "I don't have time" → …
- "I already tried X" → …
- "Is it beginner-friendly?" → …

## 8. Pricing

- Price + what compares to (DIY hours, course cost)
- Guarantee / refund policy summary
- FAQ accordion (link to fuller FAQ)

## 9. Final CTA

- Repeat outcome + CTA button
- Support email
